# What is deliberately not in this archive, and why

A package that is silent about its gaps leaves a reader unable to tell "absent" from
"hidden". Each item below is a decision, not an oversight.

## 1. Raw speech recordings of the author's voice — excluded

**What:** 31 WAV files of the author reading the ten-sentence test set (2026-09-06), plus
the ten synthesised-voice files from 2026-09-04, plus every per-utterance WAV written by
the live pipeline.

**Why:** a voice recording is biometric, identifiable personal data. The author is the only
speaker and consented to the *measurement*, but this deposit is a permanent, world-readable
publication under a DOI that cannot be withdrawn, and no written consent covering that was
obtained. Consent for a lab measurement is not consent for permanent publication.

**What is included instead:** the reference transcripts, every derived measurement, the
scoring scripts, and the word-error-rate outputs per utterance. Anyone can verify the
scoring arithmetic and every claim computed from it.

**What this costs the reader — stated plainly because it is a real limit on
reproducibility:** the speech-recognition layer cannot be re-run end to end from this
archive. A re-analyst can check how we scored the transcripts; they cannot check that the
recogniser produces those transcripts from that audio. That is a genuine gap in a paper
that argues for reproducibility, and we would rather name it than paper over it.

**How to lift it:** on request to the author, with a data-use agreement, for research use.

**Status under preregistration AMENDMENT 04 (2026-09-17):** unchanged. Tier **S1** (the 31
real-voice files, re-run) and tier **S2** (110 in-the-wild files from live sessions) stay
excluded for the reason above.

## 1b. Synthetic speech, tier S3 — INCLUDED (the exception, and why it is safe)

**What:** the S3 tier of the ASR repeat study — 23 sentences × 3 synthetic voices × 3
renders (≥ 200 WAV files, 16 kHz mono PCM16), under `data/stt/s3/`, with
`M1_s3_manifest.json` giving the SHA-256 of every file, the TTS engines and their file
hashes, the voices, and the VAD check each file passed.

**Why it can be published when item 1 cannot:** no human voice is recorded. The audio is
generated by two open text-to-speech models (VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo for Vietnamese, Kokoro-82M
for English), both Apache-2.0; the VieNeu model card states that its bundled preset voices
were provided with consent for synthetic speech generation and that audio generated from them
may be used, including commercially. Only preset voices were used — no voice cloning.

**Licence:** CC BY 4.0, like the other measurement data; credit the two TTS models.

**What it buys the reader:** the hosted-versus-local repeat measurement can be re-run end to
end on byte-identical inputs. **What it does not buy:** any statement about human speech —
synthetic speech is cleaner and more regular (ADR-004 §4), so S3 tests only whether the
delegated backend changes its output on a fixed input; it is not an accuracy benchmark.

**Mechanism:** the package builder still refuses every `.wav`; the only exception is a file
under `data/stt/s3/` whose SHA-256 matches an accepted entry of `M1_s3_manifest.json`.

## 2. Credentials and configuration — excluded

`.env`, API keys, tokens, hotspot credentials, device identifiers beyond those already in
the logs. The packaging script uses an **allow-list** of paths rather than a deny-list of
patterns, and additionally scans every file for key-shaped strings before writing anything;
it aborts on any hit rather than redacting.

## 3. Firmware binaries and vendor source — excluded

The firmware is a third-party project plus a board-support layer. Binaries are large, are
rebuildable from the recorded configuration, and are not evidence for any claim. The
board-support sources that *are* ours live in the code repository.

## 4. The response-language A/B of 2026-09-06 — does not exist

Not withheld: **lost**. The six-condition comparison behind one design decision wrote no
file that survives. Working logs, the auxiliary study directory and the full latency
archive were all searched. Two of the six conditions additionally depended on a
memory-store state that is not version-controlled and has since changed, so a re-run would
be a new measurement, not a replication.

The paper therefore prints no number for it and the layer table carries an empty cell. It
is listed here so a reader who goes looking stops looking.

## 5. Intermediate audio of the downlink measurement — excluded by size

The three acoustic recordings behind the downlink figures are ~100 MB each of 4-channel
48 kHz audio, and they contain the author's voice (see item 1). The per-turn analysis
extracted from them — click marks, onset marks, correlation peaks and margins — is included
in full as `devout_20260910_p{1,2,3}.json`, which is what the tables are computed from.

## 6. Anything under `docs/` that is not a protocol or a ledger

Internal decision records, session notes and planning documents are working material, not
evidence. Where one of them is the sole support for a claim in the paper, the paper says so
in the text rather than relying on this archive.
